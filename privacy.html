Privacy Policy

01 — Introduction

Luxury Privé ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. Luxury Privé operates as an independent boutique concierge and lifestyle coordination agency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or engage our coordination and concierge services.

This policy is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and applicable Czech and Italian data protection laws.

Data Controller: Luxury Privé
Contact: concierge.luxuryprive@gmail.com

02 — Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data in order to coordinate your requests:

  • Contact Information — name, email address, phone number provided via our enquiry form or email correspondence.
  • Service Preferences & Logistics — itinerary details, destination choices, travel dates, dietary restrictions, or specific service requirements necessary to facilitate concierge coordination.
  • Communication Data — correspondence you send to us regarding lifestyle requests or consultations.
  • Usage Data — technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our website (via Google Analytics).
  • Cookies — small data files placed on your device to improve browsing experience and enable analytics.

03 — How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and coordinate concierge, travel, and lifestyle services with third-party providers on your behalf.
  • To manage client relationships and deliver tailored lifestyle assistance.
  • To communicate itinerary plans, confirmations, updates, and coordination details.
  • To improve our website performance and user experience through aggregated analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations and safeguard our legitimate business interests.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for independent marketing purposes.

04 — Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity & pre-contractual steps — to fulfill and coordinate concierge service requests you have initiated.
  • Legitimate interests — to manage communications, connect clients with suitable external partners, and optimize our boutique services.
  • Consent — where you have explicitly opted in (e.g., website cookies or voluntary disclosure of special preferences).
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable statutory laws.

05 — Sharing Data with Independent Partners

Because Luxury Privé acts as a coordination intermediary, fulfilling your requests often requires sharing specific personal information (e.g., name, travel details, reservation preferences) with vetted third-party service providers (such as hospitality venues, private chefs, charter suppliers, or transport operators).

Data is shared solely to the extent necessary to execute the requested arrangement. Third-party partners process your data under their own independent privacy policies and terms of service.

06 — Google Analytics & Cookies

Our website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand visitor traffic. Collected data (such as page views and session duration) is anonymized and aggregated.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on, or by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

07 — Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the coordination purpose for which it was collected or to satisfy statutory record-keeping requirements. Inquiry and communication records are generally retained for up to 3 years from the date of last contact unless erasure is requested.

08 — Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

09 — Your Rights Under GDPR

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification — to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your personal records ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we utilize your information.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate business interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time where processing relies on your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at concierge.luxuryprive@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
